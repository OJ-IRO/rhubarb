# Rhubarb voice-over (words only)

Rhubarb is a tutor that listens through your Bee and teaches you from your own life. Wear it
through your day, and every conversation becomes something to learn from. A clerk you didn't
follow. A station announcement you half caught. A sentence you tried to say and couldn't finish.
Each moment is pinned to where it happened: a landmark or a neighbourhood, never your address.

But the real challenge is trust. Bee can mishear, and it doesn't always know who said what. Build
a lesson on a bad guess, and you teach the wrong thing. So every evening, Rhubarb shows you what it
thinks happened, and you mark it right or not quite. Nothing gets built until you say so.

From what you confirm, it suggests what to learn. Say yes, say how much you already know, and pick
how much reading help you want.

Every lesson is built around your own words: the exact line the clerk said to you, checked by a
second AI before you ever see it.

And Rhubarb talks back through the Bee. Practice arrives as an alarm on your phone, and you answer
out loud into the Bee. A new plan, a finished lesson, a skill you used for real: your phone buzzes.

Curious about something? Just ask out loud. Ride the Ferris wheel in Osaka and ask how it got
there. Bee heard "Osaka" as "Old Southfield." Fix one word, and the lesson on that exact wheel is
waiting when you get back down. Smaller questions become sparks: a quick answer, but you guess first.

The real test comes later. Use what you learned in a real conversation, days after the lesson, and
Rhubarb listens for it. Every skill grows in your honeycomb, and real use is what counts. Not a
streak. Not time in an app.

Look back at any day to see what you heard and what came of it. Private moments are dropped before
any AI sees them. Text only, never audio. And you can forget a moment, a day, or everything.

Built on Bee's live stream with Claude, and open source.

This is Rhubarb, where your day is the lesson, and it hears you get better. Thank you for your time.

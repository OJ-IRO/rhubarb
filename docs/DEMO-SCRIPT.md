# Rhubarb demo video: screen recording with a voice-over

Target length **2:30** (the rule is under 3:00). Everything is a screen recording of your **real
Rhubarb** on Railway, plus a few seconds of your phone. No street filming. English voice-over;
any Japanese on screen gets an English subtitle.

The Bee track's hard rule: the video must show the project using **real data from a real Bee**.
A screen recording of your own Rhubarb does that, as long as the real moments are on screen:
the listening light, what Bee heard, your days and places. Never record the Vercel demo for this.

---

## Before you record: make these true first

1. **Say yes to the tax-free plan.** Learning → "免税 shopping: asking for tax-free at the
   register" → **Yes** → **Know some** → your reading help. The lesson arrives in a few minutes.
2. **Do the lesson,** quiz included. On "Teach it back", tap **Send to my Bee** and answer the
   alarm out loud into your Bee.
3. **Use it for real once.** At a shop, ask 「免税できますか」 and follow where they send you.
   Within about 15 minutes your phone should buzz: "Heard you use…". **Take a screenshot of that
   alarm.** If it doesn't come, tell the build session; it can check what Bee heard.
4. **Look over one evening review** (Your day → Look it over). Record this one live, below.

## Recording setup

- **Mac:** QuickTime → File → New Screen Recording → record a window. Browser zoom 125%, one
  tab, light mode, close notifications. Move the mouse slowly; pause on each screen for a beat.
- **Phone:** the practice alarm and the "Heard you use…" alarm, as screen recordings or
  screenshots (Control Centre → Screen Recording).
- **Voice-over:** record it separately afterwards, reading the lines below, then lay it over the
  clips (iMovie or CapCut is enough). Recording the voice last means you never redo a screen
  take because you stumbled on a word.
- Optional but good: a 3-second clip or photo of the Bee on your wrist for the opening.

## The script

### 0:00 – 0:15 · Hook
**Screen:** photo or clip of the Bee on your wrist, then Your day with the listening light
showing "Listening · heard you … ago".
**Voice-over:**
> I'm learning Japanese in Osaka. Every day I have the exact conversations I'm trying to learn,
> and by dinner they're gone. Rhubarb listens through my Bee and turns my own day into lessons.

### 0:15 – 0:35 · What Bee heard
**Screen:** Looking back → pick Thu Oct 1 in the dropdown. Pause on "822 lines heard",
"Hakata, Fukuoka", and scroll a few real moments (the station announcement, the tax-free line).
**Voice-over:**
> This is a real day. My Bee heard eight hundred lines: station announcements in Hakata, a
> clerk at a tax-free counter, me asking a shop for something I didn't know the word for.
> Rhubarb keeps the words, never the audio, and picks out the moments worth learning from.

### 0:35 – 1:00 · It checks before it assumes
**Screen:** Your day → "Look it over" → the tax-free finding → **Right** → next finding →
"What Bee heard" → **Not quite**, type a short correction → Done.
**Voice-over:**
> Every evening it shows me what it thinks happened. Speech recognition mishears, and it can't
> tell my voice from anyone else's, so it asks first. I confirm what's true and fix what it got
> wrong. Nothing gets built on a mishearing.

### 1:00 – 1:30 · A lesson made from my own words
**Screen:** Learning → the tax-free plan → open the lesson. Pause on the card that quotes the
clerk's sentence, the floors card, then "What was checked" with the fact-check line.
**Voice-over:**
> Then it suggests what to learn, and when I say yes, it writes a ten-minute lesson around the
> exact sentence the clerk said to me. A second AI reads it for factual mistakes before I see it,
> and anything it isn't sure of, it tells me.

### 1:30 – 1:45 · Practice out loud
**Screen:** phone: the practice alarm on the lock screen. Then the graded answer in the app.
**Voice-over:**
> Practice arrives on my phone. I answer out loud into the Bee, and it tells me what I got right
> and the one thing to add.

### 1:45 – 2:10 · It hears me get better
**Screen:** phone: the "Heard you use…" alarm. Then Looking back showing **Used it for real**,
then Learning with the tax-free cell in the honeycomb filling.
**Voice-over:**
> A few days later I asked for tax-free at a different shop, on my own. Rhubarb heard it and
> counted it. Not a quiz score or a streak: real Japanese, used for real, is the progress.

### 2:10 – 2:30 · Trust, and how it's built
**Screen:** Settings → Privacy and safety. End on the GitHub repo page.
**Voice-over:**
> It keeps text, never audio. Private moments are dropped before any AI sees them, and I can
> forget a moment, a day, or everything. Rhubarb is built on Bee's live stream and CLI with
> Claude, and it's open source.

**End card (3 s):** Rhubarb · It hears you get better · github.com/OJ-IRO/rhubarb

## If it runs long, cut in this order

1. The practice beat (1:30 – 1:45).
2. The "Not quite" correction in the review (keep the Right tap).
3. Shorten the "What Bee heard" scroll.

Never cut "It hears me get better" (1:45 – 2:10). It's the thing no other entry has.
